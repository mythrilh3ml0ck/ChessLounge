Privacy & Security Protocol

Mission Statement

Secret Site is dedicated to providing a secure and private environment for users. Our architecture is built on the principle of "Privacy by Design," ensuring that your digital footprint remains entirely under your control.

Zero-Knowledge Policy

We operate under a strict Zero-Knowledge framework. This means:

  • No personal identification data is collected.
  • No registration or account creation is required.
  • No activity logs or usage metadata are stored.
  • We cannot see, access, or share your content.

Permission Transparency

Secret Site only requests the absolute minimum permissions required for operation. We explicitly DO NOT access:

  • Biometric or personal identity data
  • Contact lists or social connections
  • Continuous background location tracking
  • Private media libraries without intent

Data Integrity

Your data is encrypted using industry-standard protocols. Since we do not maintain centralized servers for user data, there is no single point of failure or data breach risk from our end. Your device is your vault.

Third-Party Integrity

We do not partner with advertising networks or data brokers. There are no tracking pixels, analytics cookies, or third-party SDKs hidden within the application.

Policy Governance
