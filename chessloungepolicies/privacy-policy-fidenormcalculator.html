FIDE Norm Calculator • August 19, 2026

Introduction

FIDE Norm Calculator is part of the Chess Lounge Series developed under The Lounge. This application is a professional utility designed to help chess players, coaches, and arbiters calculate and validate title norms (GM, IM, WGM, WIM) according to the FIDE Handbook regulations.

Information Collection & Usage

Personal Data: The application does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII). There is no user registration or login system.

Calculation Data: Any data you enter—such as player names, ratings, and tournament results—is stored locally on your device in a private database. This allows you to view your calculation history offline. This data is never uploaded to any servers and is accessible only to you through the application.

Permissions

The application requests specific permissions to provide its features:

  • Internet Access: Used exclusively for displaying advertisements via Google AdMob and accessing the online FIDE Handbook.
  • Network State: Used to check for connectivity to load external resources.

Third-Party Services

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device-specific information (such as your device ID) and usage data to serve relevant ads. This data collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Disclaimer

This application is a validation tool and does not officially award FIDE titles or norms. All results should be verified with official FIDE regulations and national federations.
