Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 13, 2026

Introduction

Sudoku Solver is a puzzle utility developed by The Lounge as part of the Game Lounge Series. The application helps users solve Sudoku puzzles quickly and accurately by calculating the correct solution from the numbers entered.

🧩 Simple • Offline • Private

Your Sudoku puzzles are solved directly on your device. Nothing is uploaded or shared.

Information Collection

Sudoku Solver does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. No registration, login, or account is required to use the application.

All Sudoku boards entered into the app are processed locally on your device.

Device Permissions

Sudoku Solver does not request access to:

  • 🎤 Microphone
  • 📍 Location
  • 👥 Contacts
  • 🖼 Photos or Media
  • 📁 Personal Files

The application operates without accessing sensitive device information.

Internet Usage

Sudoku Solver is fully functional offline. An internet connection is not required to solve puzzles, and no user or puzzle information is transmitted online.

Third-Party Services

The application does not use advertisements, analytics platforms, or third-party tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Because Sudoku Solver does not collect personal information, it is appropriate for users of all ages, including children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer.
