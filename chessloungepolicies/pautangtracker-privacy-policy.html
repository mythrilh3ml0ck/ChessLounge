Privacy Policy

Pautang Tracker • Effective Date: Jan 1, 2026

Thank you for using Pautang Tracker. We respect your privacy and are committed to ensuring that your personal and financial information is protected. This application is designed to operate primarily offline, and we do NOT collect, transmit, or store your data on any external servers.

1. No Data Collection by Developer

Pautang Tracker does not collect any of your personal or financial data. All information you enter into the app remains strictly on your device. The developer has zero access to:

  • Your name, username, or mobile number.
  • The names or contact details of your borrowers.
  • Your loan amounts, payment history, or any other financial records.
  • Any photos or documents you attach to your records.

2. Information Stored Locally

To provide its core features, the application saves the following information locally on your device's internal storage:

  • Account Security: Your encrypted MPIN and recovery code.
  • Borrower Profiles: Information about people you track (names, nicknames, addresses, etc.).
  • Loan Records: Detailed logs of money lent, borrowed, and payments made.
  • Media Attachments: Images or documents attached to specific entries for your personal reference.

3. Data Security and Privacy

Local Storage: Your data is stored in the app's private directory, which is not accessible by other applications.

Encrypted MPIN: Your access code is encrypted to ensure that only you can open the app.

Full User Control: You have absolute control over your data. You can delete specific records, reset your entire account, or clear all app data at any time through the App Settings. Once deleted, this information is gone forever and cannot be recovered by the developer.

4. Third-Party Services

To support the application, we use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements. AdMob may use device identifiers (like Advertising IDs) to serve relevant ads. This data is handled by Google and is not shared with the developer.
  • Google Glide: Used locally for displaying images (like profile pictures) within the app.

5. Permissions

The application requests only the permissions necessary for its features:

  • Notifications: To send you alerts for due dates and reminders.
  • Camera/Storage: To allow you to take or select photos for your records.
  • Internet: Required only for showing advertisements and optional external links.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally. Any changes will be reflected here with an updated effective date.
