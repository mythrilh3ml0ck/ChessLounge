Privacy Policy

Baybayin Translator
Effective Date: January 1, 2025

Thank you for using Baybayin Translator. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the application collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

Baybayin Translator is designed to function primarily offline. We do not collect personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily provide it.

The application may temporarily access:

  • Camera (for Baybayin image recognition)
  • Images selected from your device
  • Translation history stored locally
  • Favorite translations stored locally

All stored data remains on your device unless you choose to share it.

2. How We Use Information

The application uses information solely to provide its features, including:

  • Romanized Filipino ↔ Baybayin translation
  • Camera-based Baybayin recognition
  • Saving translation history
  • Managing favorite translations
  • Sharing translations upon user request

3. Data Storage

Translation history, favorites, and application settings are stored locally on your device. The developer does not receive or store this information.

4. Internet Access

Most features of Baybayin Translator work completely offline. If future versions include optional online services, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Permissions

The application may request the following permissions:

  • Camera – Used only for recognizing Baybayin characters through the camera.
  • Photos/Storage – Used only when you choose an image for recognition or sharing.

Permissions are only used for their intended purpose and are never used to collect personal information.

6. Third-Party Services

Baybayin Translator does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. If future versions integrate third-party services, they will be disclosed in this policy.

7. Children's Privacy

Baybayin Translator is suitable for educational use by users of all ages. The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Security

We strive to keep your information secure by storing your data locally on your device. However, no storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Disclaimer

Baybayin transliterations and interpretations may vary depending on historical conventions, regional practices, and modern adaptations. The translations provided are intended for educational and informational purposes only and should not be considered definitive.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page together with the updated effective date.

11. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer through the official project channels.
