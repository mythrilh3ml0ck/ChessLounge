Chess Lounge Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 14, 2026

Introduction

Chess Lounge is an informational application designed to provide chess-related resources and rating lists for Filipino chess players. The app allows users to view publicly available chess ratings and reference tools for players and arbiters.

Information Collection

Chess Lounge does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information from users. The application does not require user registration, login, or personal data input.

Device Permissions

Chess Lounge does not request or use access to sensitive device features such as:

  • Microphone
  • Camera
  • Location services
  • Contacts
  • Personal file storage

The app does not record audio, capture images, or access private user data.

Internet Usage

The application may use an internet connection to retrieve and display publicly available chess rating lists and related chess information. This data is used solely for informational purposes and is not connected to any personal user identity.

Third-Party Services

Chess Lounge does not use advertising networks, analytics tools, or third-party tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Since the application does not collect any personal information, it is safe for users of all ages, including children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer.
