Chess Lounge's Chess Clock Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 14, 2026

Introduction

Chess Lounge's Chess Clock is a digital chess clock application designed to facilitate timed chess matches. The app provides customizable time controls and increments for players, serving as a reliable tool for over-the-board play.

Information Collection

Chess Lounge's Chess Clock does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information from users. The application does not require user registration, login, or personal data input. All settings and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Device Permissions

Chess Lounge's Chess Clock does not request or use access to sensitive device features such as:

  • Microphone
  • Camera
  • Location services
  • Contacts
  • Personal file storage

The app does not record audio, capture images, or access private user data. It only requires standard hardware acceleration and screen-on (Wake Lock) permissions to ensure the clock runs smoothly during gameplay.

Internet Usage

The application operates primarily offline. While it may use a basic internet connection for technical assets, no personal user identity or usage data is transmitted.

Third-Party Services

Chess Lounge's Chess Clock does not use advertising networks, analytics tools, or third-party tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Since the application does not collect any personal information, it is safe for users of all ages, including children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer.
