Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 20, 2026

The Philippine Chess Tournament app (the "App") is powered by The Lounge under the Chess Lounge Series. The App is designed to provide users with information about chess tournaments in the Philippines. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

The App is designed to be privacy-friendly. It does not require user accounts, email addresses, or any personal identification details.

  • Local Data: When you bookmark a tournament, that information is stored locally on your device using a secure database. This data never leaves your device.
  • Automatically Collected Info: The App does not automatically collect any personally identifiable information (PII). It fetches public tournament data from third-party websites to display to you.

2. Use of Information

Any information stored by the App is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To maintain your list of bookmarked tournaments.
  • To provide notifications about upcoming tournaments you have bookmarked.
  • To display current and upcoming chess events.

3. Third-Party Links

The App contains links to third-party websites (such as tournament organizer pages) and services (such as Google Maps). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any website you visit.

4. Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

  • Internet: Used to fetch the latest tournament information.
  • Notifications: Used to alert you about new tournaments or starting dates for your bookmarks.

5. Data Security

Since your bookmarks are stored locally on your device, their security depends on your device's security. We do not transmit your personal preferences or bookmarks to any external servers.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the App.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us through the official App store page.
