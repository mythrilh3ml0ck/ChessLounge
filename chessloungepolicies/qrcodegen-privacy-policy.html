Privacy Policy

QR Code Generator
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Thank you for using QR Code Generator. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the application collects, uses, and protects your information while using our services.

1. Information We Collect

QR Code Generator is designed to operate primarily on your device. We do not collect personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily provide it.

The application may temporarily access:

  • Camera (for scanning QR Codes and Barcodes)
  • Images selected from your device for scanning
  • Generated QR Codes saved locally
  • Scan history stored locally (if enabled)
  • Application preferences and settings

All information remains on your device unless you choose to export or share it.

2. How We Use Information

The application uses information solely to provide its features, including:

  • Generating standard QR Codes
  • Generating custom Chess-themed QR Codes
  • Scanning QR Codes and Barcodes
  • Saving generated codes and scan history locally
  • Sharing QR Codes at your request

3. Data Storage

Generated QR Codes, scan history, and application settings are stored locally on your device. The developer does not receive, upload, or store your personal data.

4. Internet Access

Most features of QR Code Generator work completely offline. Internet access may only be required for optional future features or when opening links contained within scanned QR Codes.

5. Permissions

The application may request the following permissions:

  • Camera – Used only for scanning QR Codes and Barcodes.
  • Photos/Storage – Used only when selecting or saving QR Code images.

Permissions are used only for their intended functionality and are never used to collect personal information.

6. Third-Party Services

QR Code Generator does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Should future versions integrate optional third-party services, they will be clearly disclosed in updated versions of this Privacy Policy.

7. Children's Privacy

QR Code Generator is suitable for users of all ages. The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Security

We prioritize your privacy by keeping your information on your own device whenever possible. Although we strive to protect your data, no storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Disclaimer

QR Code Generator provides tools for creating and scanning QR Codes and Barcodes. Users are responsible for the content they encode or access through generated or scanned codes. The optional Chess-themed QR Code designs are decorative and intended to enhance visual appearance without affecting QR Code functionality.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any revisions will be published on this page together with the updated effective date.

11. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer through the official project channels.
