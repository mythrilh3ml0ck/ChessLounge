Privacy Policy

Introduction

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how "Visualization Games" (part of the Chess Lounge Series) handles your data.

Data Collection

Personal Information: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal identification information (such as your name, email address, or phone number).

Local Storage: The application uses local storage on your device to save your leaderboard scores and player name. This data remains on your device and is never uploaded to our servers.

Offline Functionality

Visualization Games is designed to function entirely offline. No internet connection is required for core training modules, and no usage data is tracked or shared with third parties.

Permissions

This app requires minimal permissions to operate, primarily focusing on displaying the training interface and saving your local progress.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding this policy, you may contact us through the official Chess Lounge channels.
